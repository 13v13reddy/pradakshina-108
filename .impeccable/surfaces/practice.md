# Practice screen

## Scope and mode
Operate. Mobile-first single-screen practice, a round history sheet, and a preferences sheet. iPhone is the primary device.

## Audience and task
A temple visitor completing 108 pradakshinas who may forget whether a round was recorded. The main action must save a round deliberately and leave a lasting, timestamped receipt.

## Chosen direction
Seed a3910210 assigns candidate 6: temple pond. A committed pond-green field, brass details, jasmine text, an expressive but restrained serif, and a circular sequence of 108 marks. The actual controls stay standard web buttons and sheets. The visual world contributes palette, type, density, and one signature interaction.

Grounded candidates considered: japa mala; kolam linework; stone inscriptions; palm-leaf manuscripts; flower offering baskets; temple pond; festival print programmes. Temple pond is sixth. The cassette and catalogue challengers lose audience identification; the manual and pixel specimen introduce reading friction; the numbered record sleeve and emission rail obscure the practical count. All declined. Their discipline raises the chosen direction through aligned timestamp columns, clear completed/pending marks, a single memorable field, and consistent state grammar.

## FIRST VIEWPORT
Mobile: restrained header, a short reassuring title, a generous 108-bead progress circle around the count, a persistent latest-round receipt, and a large hold button within thumb reach. Desktop: the same functional practice column, with a calm introductory column to its left. No fake counts or synthetic practice history.

## SIGNATURE INTERACTION
An 850ms press fills the primary control and commits exactly one round. The next bead fills, a saved-round receipt changes visibly, and a 12-second guard prevents a second record. The receipt keeps its exact time and a relative age after the animation ends. Cancelling a hold does not count. Returning to the app recovers both the count and receipt.

## Layout, type, and color
An open green surface, no nested card scaffolding. Instrument Serif for the expressive heading and count; DM Sans for controls, timestamps, and explanations. Green #153e35; deeper green #0f3029; jasmine #f6f3e8; sage #b8cbbd; brass #e0bd7f; ink #173d34. Local font assets.

## States and resilience
Empty, saved, duplicate-guarded, paused, 108 complete, offline-ready, save failure, and corrupt data. Keyboard/assistive activation, reduced motion, safe areas, large touch targets, timestamped history, protected undo/reset, and an accessible tap mode. No sound by default. No automatic movement detection.

## Constraints and decisions
Build directly in static HTML/CSS/JS for GitHub Pages. No raster illustration is needed for this working screen; the circle is functional SVG geometry. No standing workflow preference is stored. Publish only app assets; product/design briefs remain source documentation.
